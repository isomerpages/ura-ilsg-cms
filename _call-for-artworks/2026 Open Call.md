<h2>i Light Singapore 2026 Open Call</h2>
<p>i Light Singapore 2026 invites artists and creatives across Asia to submit
proposals for light art installations for our 12th edition.</p>
<p>As Asia’s leading light festival, i Light Singapore aims to be the region’s
premier platform for discovering, nurturing, and showcasing the next generation
of light artists — sparking creativity, collaboration, and cultural exchange
through light.</p>
<p>This edition’s curatorial theme, Movement, calls for innovative light
art installations that embody motion and transformation — exploring how
light can flow, shift, and connect us through the rhythms of our world.</p>
<p>Be part of the collective that transforms Marina Bay and other precincts
into a luminous journey of art and ideas, moving communities through light.</p>
<p>For more information, download the <a href="/files/i_Light_Singapore_2026___Open_Call_for_Asian_Artists_compressed.pdf" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank">Open Call document</a>.</p>
<p>Fill in the <a href="https://forms.gle/SpTa69bKkWoufPhw7" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank">Open Call application form</a> and
submit your proposal by <strong>11 December 2025, 2359hr GMT+8</strong>.</p>
